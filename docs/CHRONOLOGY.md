# CZŁOWIEK ROKU — CHRONOLOGY

## PURPOSE

Chronology control for the film adaptation. Dates and sequence positions below are derived from screenplay scene headings and established project materials.

## 2023

### December 2023 — SCENA 1
Adam Mickiewicz Theatre in Cieszyn.

Andrzej appears publicly during the "Człowiek Roku" ceremony. The film begins with the contrast between public prestige and the private rupture that is about to become the central story.

## 2024 — THE BREAK / INSTITUTIONAL CONFLICT

### February 2024
- Kiddy Park / family context.
- Escalation of conflict.
- Contact with Zuzia becomes a central issue.

### Late February–March 2024
- Police and administrative procedures.
- Interrogation and district officer sequences.
- Zuzia's room / memories.
- Żory / Aquapark sequence.
- Growing isolation of Andrzej.
- Church / Dom Narodowy / school-related sequences.

### Early April 2024
- Andrzej's foundation period.
- Hostel / social-support environment.
- Medical crisis.

### 2 April 2024 — SCENA 7
Intensive care.

Andrzej wakes after a severe physical collapse. Wiesław is present. The hospital becomes a visual and emotional threshold.

### 3–5 April 2024
- Mediator.
- Return to the apartment.
- Police interrogation.
- Fingerprints / institutional processing.
- Interdisciplinary team concerning domestic violence.

### Mid/Late April 2024
- Continued isolation at the foundation.
- Stairwell sequence.
- Ten-day montage.
- Preparation for the photographic counter-narrative.

### 1 May 2024
EkoTradycja, night before the exhibition.

Andrzej, Artur, Wiktoria and Julka install the new exhibition.

### 2 May 2024 — SCENA 10D
Weronika's 40th birthday and the "Projekt W." exhibition.

The exhibition becomes a public reversal: the expected Weronika narrative is replaced by portraits of Wiktoria.

### May 2024
First major court proceedings.

### June 2024
Family court sequence. The conflict is increasingly processed through legal language.

### July 2024
Restaurant / memory sequences.

### 30 July 2024 — SCENA 12F
Gallery in the Czech Republic.

Andrzej presents the "Sceny z życia" photographic cycle. The exhibition provides one of the strongest visual contrasts in the screenplay: a respected artist standing inside a controlled white gallery while his private life is collapsing elsewhere.

The screenplay explicitly places photographs of miners, elderly women and other documentary subjects on the walls. Andrzej speaks about documentary photography as finding the one moment that tells the whole story — the one second of truth.

### September 2024
Warsaw / media and public dimension.

### Autumn 2024
"Cyrk medialny" — television appearances after the fathers' protest.

### Autumn/Winter 2024
"Gra w porozumienie" — another attempt to reach an agreement.

## 2025 — THE SYSTEM CLOSES

### January 2025
Legal office / continued institutional conflict.

### February 2025
Writer sequence.

Writing increasingly becomes a tool for testimony and public action.

### May 2025
Dom Narodowy in Cieszyn.

### 17 June 2025
Court proceedings.

### 19 June 2025
Ice-cream shop / park sequence.

### Summer 2025
Further family-court proceedings.

### Summer 2025
Deep-night sequence in Andrzej's foundation room.

### End of June 2025
School sequence.

### Summer 2025
OZSS / specialist court assessment.

Zuzia's examination is treated as a major psychological and institutional pressure point.

### Several weeks later
Andrzej moves into a new apartment.

### October 2025
New apartment.

### October 2025 — SCENA 19A
Breakdown montage.

Andrzej cannot eat or sleep. He watches the empty children's room. The apartment is physically new but emotionally functions as a tomb-like space.

## FINAL MOVEMENT

The final legal/institutional decision is communicated as the confirmation of an adverse narrative. Andrzej's own words are used against him.

He is left with writing, memory and the photograph of Zuzia.

The final shutter sound closes the film.

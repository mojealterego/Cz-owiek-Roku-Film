# ADAPTATION OPTION — TOMEK'S WEDDING

## STATUS

**PROPOSED ADAPTATION / NOT CANONICAL**

This document records a production idea raised during character/reference development. It does not alter the 74-scene screenplay map until explicitly approved.

## CORE IDEA

Add a wedding scene involving Tomek and his wife.

### Dramaturgical mechanism

**Andrzej = wedding photographer.**

**Tomek = tattoo artist.**

The photography work performed by Andrzej at the wedding is used as the practical **settlement/payment for Tomek's tattoo work**.

This creates a direct exchange between the two professions rather than introducing an arbitrary financial transaction.

## WHY THE SCENE HAS VALUE

### 1. It gives Tomek's wife a legitimate on-screen reason to exist
The woman already supplied as Tomek's wife does not currently appear in the film. A wedding makes her presence organic rather than creating a gratuitous character scene.

### 2. It connects Andrzej's two worlds
Andrzej is simultaneously:
- a photographer;
- a man undergoing a physical/identity transformation through tattooing.

The wedding lets his professional skill directly facilitate the transformation.

### 3. It can work as visual counterpoint
A wedding is conventionally associated with:
- celebration;
- promises;
- photographs;
- family;
- permanence;
- public happiness.

Andrzej can be professionally documenting another couple's milestone while his own family life is collapsing. This contrast is potentially strong, but it must be written into the scene rather than assumed.

### 4. It can reinforce the photography motif
The camera inside the film can photograph the wedding while the film camera observes Andrzej photographing it. This creates a natural **frame-within-frame** structure consistent with the project's photography language.

## POSSIBLE VISUAL TREATMENT — FILM DECISION, NOT YET CANON

If approved, the scene could use two layers:

**PUBLIC LAYER:** wedding celebration, guests, ceremony/reception, photographs, movement, music, smiles.

**ANDRZEJ'S SUBJECTIVE LAYER:** selective observation through the camera, moments of emotional distance, faces seen through the viewfinder, flashes, brief isolation inside a crowded room.

The wedding should not become a glossy music-video sequence. It must remain inside the established grounded Polish visual language.

## TATTOO CONTINUITY

The wedding's chronological position must be checked against the tattoo timeline.

If the wedding is used as payment for tattoo work:
- the relevant tattoo work must occur at the correct point in the chronology;
- the back tattoo state must remain **Weronika portrait** until the transformation point;
- no Santa Muerte version may appear before the transformation;
- if the wedding is placed after the transformation, the post-transformation tattoo state must be used;
- if placed before transformation, the original Weronika portrait must be used.

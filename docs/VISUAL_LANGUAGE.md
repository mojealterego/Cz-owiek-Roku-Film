# VISUAL LANGUAGE — CZŁOWIEK ROKU

## Core formula

**Smarzowski = rzeczywistość**  
**Żulczyk / Ślepnąc od świateł = psychika i noc**  
**Nick Love / Football Factory = ulica i męska energia**

To nie są trzy osobne style. To jedna filmowa gramatyka.

## Realism

- dirty realism;
- naturalistic faces;
- practical-looking environments;
- fluorescent interiors;
- wet asphalt;
- cheap bars;
- stairwells;
- offices and institutions;
- courts and police rooms;
- cars;
- paper files;
- old phones;
- background extras with their own lives;
- imperfections rather than polished advertising imagery.

## Night language

- black sky;
- cold streetlights;
- isolated neon;
- wet reflections;
- car-window reflections;
- blue phone/monitor glow;
- psychological isolation;
- slow/hypnotic visual transitions where appropriate.

## Street language

- tighter framing;
- nervous camera where dramatic energy demands it;
- physicality;
- male group dynamics;
- sudden escalation;
- no stylized action for its own sake.

## Camera as psychological instrument

### Early Andrzej
- controlled composition;
- geometry;
- symmetry;
- deliberate framing;
- clean lines.

### Later Andrzej
- obstruction;
- reflections;
- dirty foreground;
- imperfect focus;
- camera too close;
- characters entering/leaving frame;
- unstable visual control.

The image gradually becomes less controlled as Andrzej's life becomes less controlled.

## Photography motif

The camera shutter is a recurring sound motif:
- first: real photographic action;
- later: memory and obsession;
- finale: almost a last breath.

## Image generation baseline

Default feature-film baseline:

`CINEMATIC LIVE-ACTION FEATURE FILM, grounded Polish drama, photorealistic human anatomy, natural skin texture, physically plausible lighting, subtle film grain, restrained production design, 2.39:1 anamorphic composition, 35mm feature-film image language, realistic depth of field, no fantasy, no surreal CGI, no text artifacts, no watermarks, no logos, consistent character identity, continuity with preceding shot, preserve exact emotional beat from the source scene.`

## Negative baseline

Avoid:
- glossy commercial look;
- fashion editorial look unless source demands it;
- plastic skin;
- perfect teeth/faces;
- excessive HDR;
- fantasy CGI;
- random tattoos;
- random props;
- random wardrobe changes;
- inconsistent ages;
- face drift;
- text artifacts;
- fake logos;
- watermarks;
- over-stylized lens effects.
